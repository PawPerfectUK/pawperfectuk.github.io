Returns Policy

Last updated: June 2026

🐾 We offer a 30-day no-questions-asked return policy on all orders.

How to return an item

If you're not completely happy with your purchase, you can return it within 30 days of receiving it. Here's how:

  • Email us at hello@pawperfect.co.uk with your order number and reason for return
  • We'll reply within 24 hours with return instructions
  • Send the item back in its original packaging if possible
  • Once we confirm receipt, your refund is issued within 5–7 business days

What can be returned

  • Items in their original condition (unused or minimally used)
  • Items with original packaging where possible
  • Items reported within 30 days of delivery

What cannot be returned

  • Items that have been heavily used or damaged by misuse
  • Personalised or customised items (where applicable)
  • Consumables once opened

Damaged or incorrect items

If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, please email us within 7 days with a photo. We will send a replacement or issue a full refund immediately — no need to return the original item.

Refunds

Refunds are issued to your original payment method. Please allow 5–7 business days for the refund to appear, depending on your bank or card provider.

Return shipping costs

For standard returns (change of mind), return postage is the customer's responsibility. For damaged, faulty, or incorrect items, we cover all return costs.

Shipping Information

Last updated: June 2026

🚚 Free UK delivery on all orders over £30. Standard delivery £3.99.

Delivery times

We ship all orders directly from our fulfilment partner. Please allow the following timeframes:

  • Standard UK delivery: 7–14 business days
  • Expedited UK delivery: 5–8 business days (where available)
  • International orders: 10–20 business days

Delivery times are estimates and may vary during busy periods such as Christmas, Black Friday, or due to courier delays outside our control.

Shipping costs

  • UK orders under £30: £3.99 standard shipping
  • UK orders over £30: Free standard shipping
  • International shipping: Calculated at checkout

Order tracking

Once your order is dispatched, you'll receive an email with a tracking number. You can use this to track your parcel directly on the courier's website. If you don't receive a tracking email within 3 business days of ordering, please check your spam folder or contact us.

Where we ship

We currently ship to the UK, Republic of Ireland, and most of Europe. If your country isn't available at checkout, email us and we'll check if we can fulfil your order.

Customs and import duties

For international orders outside the UK, customs duties or import taxes may apply. These are the responsibility of the customer and are not included in our product prices or shipping costs.

Lost or delayed parcels

If your order hasn't arrived within 20 business days of dispatch, please contact us at hello@pawperfect.co.uk. We'll investigate immediately and either resend the order or issue a full refund.

Privacy Policy
